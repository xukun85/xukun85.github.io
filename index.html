Kun Xu 徐昆

Associate Professor · Tsinghua University

I am an associate professor in the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University. I received my Ph.D. (2009) and B.S. (2005) degrees, both from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, where I was advised by Prof. Shi-Min Hu.

My research is in computer graphics — real-time and physically based rendering, sample reuse and variance reduction for Monte Carlo rendering and PDE solvers, differentiable and neural rendering, renderer architecture, and image/video editing.
